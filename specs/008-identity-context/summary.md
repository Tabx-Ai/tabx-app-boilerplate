An app opened from the workspace was told almost nothing about the person using it: an internal identifier, the workspace, and sometimes a name. It could not show an email, a job title, or who somebody reports to, so any screen wanting those invented something. The platform now hands over the email, the name, the person's department, designation, subsidiary and role, and their immediate manager. Inside the app that identity stops being a loose bag of fields and becomes a proper object the code passes around and asks questions of, and one that cannot be faked, because only the part that validates it can build one. On screen, one reusable piece of code loads it once for the whole interface; if that fails, the affected part says so rather than the app going blank or pretending nobody is signed in.
